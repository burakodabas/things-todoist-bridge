Behaviour
Fields

Title and completion always sync. Items with the same title on both sides are linked instead of duplicated on the first run. Comments are added below your own notes in a separate “Todoist comments” block and never overwrite them.

Tasks created in Todoist

Changes to tasks that are already linked (description, dates, completion, comments) always come back to Things, whatever you pick here.

Assign to teammates

Give a to-do a tag with a teammate’s name (Sam, @sam, #Sam) or write @sam in its title, and it is assigned to them in Todoist. Tasks they hand back to you lose the tag. Only people the Todoist project is shared with can be assigned.

Tasks you send from Things are assigned to you in Todoist unless you tag someone, and come back to you when you remove the tag. Your teammate’s own unassigned tasks are left alone.

For tags that aren’t a name, e.g. design-team → Sam.
